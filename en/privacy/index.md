# Privacy Policy for Gym AI

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Gym AI is an app that helps gymnasts and coaches analyse jumps in Tumbling, Trampette and Floor. This page explains what information the app uses, why, and what rights you have.

## Who is responsible?

The data controller is Sander Jørgensen.
Contact: gymai.app.support@gmail.com

## What does the app store on your phone?

The following is stored only on your phone and does not leave it, unless stated otherwise below:

- Your videos and the clips you have selected
- Frames taken from the videos
- Your analyses and reports
- Your planned sessions, goals and drill plans in the calendar

You can delete all of this at any time in the app under **Profile → Delete all my data**. When you delete the app, the data is removed as well.

## What is sent to a server?

When you start an analysis, the app sends the following, via our server, to Anthropic, which performs the analysis using artificial intelligence:

- A selection of frames from your video (about 12 to 16 frames)
- The element, level, discipline and setting you have chosen
- Numbers from a body-pose measurement (for example whether legs and arms are straight), which is done on your phone

Your full-length video is not sent. We do not store the frames on our server. The result of the analysis is sent back to your phone and stored there.

The information is only sent if you have given consent in the app. You can withdraw your consent under **Profile → Consent**. After that, no more frames are sent.

## Technical information stored on our server

To run the service and protect it from misuse, our server stores an anonymous user ID, the number and time of your analyses, and the IP address the request came from. The IP address is used only to limit misuse (for example, too many analyses from the same network). You do not need to create an account or give your name. This information is deleted automatically after 35 days.

## Images of other people and children

Videos may show children and other people. You may only use videos where you have permission from the people in them (or their parents). You confirm this in the app before you can analyse.

The app is for gymnasts, coaches and parents. Children under 13 should only use the app together with a parent or coach.

## Who receives the information?

- **Anthropic** analyses the frames using artificial intelligence. Anthropic processes the information under its own terms: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
- **Supabase** runs the server the app communicates with, and stores an anonymous user ID, the number of analyses and the IP address described above. Read more: https://supabase.com/privacy

We do not sell information, and the app contains no advertising and no tracking for marketing.

Information may be processed outside the EU/EEA, including in the United States, by the two recipients above, under the data transfer rules they have put in place themselves.

## Legal basis

We process the frames based on your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). We use the anonymous user ID, the number of analyses and the IP address to run the service and protect it from misuse (Article 6(1)(f)).

## How long is the information kept?

- On your phone: until you delete it or delete the app.
- On our server: the number and time of your analyses and the IP address they were sent from are deleted automatically after 35 days. The anonymous user ID the app uses to count your analyses is kept for as long as you use the app.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete and restrict the processing of information about you, to receive it in a common format, and to object. You can withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before.

Write to gymai.app.support@gmail.com if you want to use your rights. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet): https://www.datatilsynet.dk

## Security

Information is sent encrypted between the app and the server.

## The analysis is an estimate

The analysis is an estimate made by artificial intelligence based on images. It is not an official judge’s assessment and may contain errors.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page, with the date at the top.
